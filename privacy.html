Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play My Pro Soccer (the "Game") on iPhone or Android, and the choices you have. The Game is developed by Emin Sıraç, an independent developer in Türkiye ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to myprosoccer1@gmail.com.

In short: there is no account and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts or photos, and we have no servers that store information about you. Your careers and settings stay on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Game saves the following on your device so that you can continue where you left off:

  • your careers (up to three save slots: player name you type, club, statistics, items, gold),
  • settings (language, sound, graphics quality),
  • purchase records (product and transaction identifiers, so that an item is never delivered twice and "Remove Ads" stays active),
  • your ad choices (for example whether ads were removed).

This information never leaves your device through us; we cannot see or access it. Deleting a career in the Game, or deleting the Game, removes it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Android backup) may keep a copy according to your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads: a full-screen ad between matches and optional reward videos (for energy) that you choose to watch. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device:

  • the device's advertising identifier (on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking; on Android the Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and technical diagnostics of the ad SDK.

Google processes this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch the Game shows Google's consent form. You can accept or refuse personalised ads, and change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options.
  • iPhone: the Game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings (Ads).
  • You can control ad personalisation in My Ad Center.
  • The "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads between matches. Reward videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for teenagers; ads for gambling, alcohol and dating are blocked.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (gold packs, items, "Remove Ads") are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never receive your payment card or account details. The store only tells the Game which product was bought, with a transaction identifier, so that the Game can deliver it; this record is kept on your device.

The casino in the Game uses in-game gold only. It never pays out real money or prizes, and gold cannot be exchanged for money.

4. No other tracking

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs, and we do not sell personal information.

5. Children

The Game is intended for ages 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and your rights

Information on your device stays until you delete it. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold information about you on servers, most requests can be fulfilled with the choices above or through Google; you are also welcome to contact us at myprosoccer1@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

7. Security

Connections the Game makes to ad and store services are encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and significant changes are noted on this page.
